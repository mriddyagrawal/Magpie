# Magpie — YC Founder Video (1 minute)

**Founders:** Rahul Ranjan Sah, Mridul
**Length:** ~60 seconds (≈147 words at ~150 wpm)
**Voice:** Conversational and warm. The detailed market case (33M/64M/24M numbers, competitor breakdown) lives in the written application — this video sounds like two people talking, not reciting.

---

## Setup

Two founders on camera, plain background, well-lit, one shared mic if possible. Eye contact with the lens. No slides.

---

## Script

**[0:00–0:07]  Cold open**

**Rahul:** Hi, I'm Rahul.
**Mridul:** I'm Mridul. We're building **Magpie** — an AI brain for your organization.

---

**[0:07–0:21]  Who we're for**

**Rahul:** Magpie is built for small businesses, and that's what we're trying to change. Most small businesses don't run on Slack or Notion. Their documents live on WhatsApp, on a phone, in scanned PDFs nobody ever filed.

---

**[0:21–0:31]  Why nobody else serves them**

**Mridul:** Every AI tool asks them to upload all of that to somebody else's cloud. Most of them won't. So they get left out — by design.

---

**[0:31–0:43]  What Magpie does**

**Rahul:** Magpie installs in one click, reads what's already on your devices, syncs across every one of them, and becomes your personal or organizational AI brain.

---

**[0:43–0:52]  The promise, made concrete**

**Mridul:** Ask it anything — *"how much did I spend on rent in March?"* Magpie answers from your own files. Nothing leaves the building.

---

**[0:52–1:00]  Close**

**Rahul:** It knows everything you have. At the end of the day, it answers back.
**Mridul:** We're Magpie.

---

**Total:** ~147 words / ~59 seconds at a relaxed 150 wpm.

---

## Delivery notes

- **Pace:** ~150 wpm. The example question (*"how much did I spend on rent in March?"*) should sound like you're actually asking it, not reciting it.
- **The two lines that have to land:**
  - *"So they get left out — by design."* — pause briefly before "by design."
  - *"Nothing leaves the building."* — your strongest line. Slow down.
- **Eye contact:** look at the lens during your own lines; look at your co-founder *only* during the handoff.
- **The handoff pattern is RMRMRM** — alternates cleanly, no awkward double-handoffs.
- **Avoid:** "passionate," "leveraging," "AI-powered," "revolutionizing," "we believe."
- **Wardrobe:** plain solid colors. Don't match (looks staged). Don't clash (looks chaotic).

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Test audio levels first — bad audio kills the video faster than bad framing.
- [ ] Record at least 3 takes; pick the one where the handoff rhythm feels natural, not the one where every word is perfect.
- [ ] Watch yourselves back on mute once — if your faces look stiff, redo it.
- [ ] Keep the file under 100 MB and under 60 seconds (YC enforces both).
