# MTH-245 Handout 0: Foundations of Statistical Inference and Data Analysis

This academic handout provides a comprehensive review of introductory statistics for an advanced course, outlining a rigorous seven-step framework for conducting data analysis from research question formulation to final inference. It distinguishes between populations and samples, parameters and statistics, and categorical versus quantitative variables, while detailing hypothesis testing methodologies—including null and alternative hypotheses, p-values, test statistics, and the critical distinction between statistical significance and effect size—and confidence interval construction. The document emphasizes the Central Limit Theorem as the mathematical foundation enabling normal sampling distributions for means and proportions, specifying conditions under which these inferential methods are valid.

**Keywords:** statistics, statistical inference, hypothesis testing, Central Limit Theorem, sampling distribution, effect size, confidence intervals, research methodology, parameters, variables
