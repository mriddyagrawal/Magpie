# Cataclysmic Variables and Related Objects: A Review

This 2012 review by Giovannelli and Sabau-Graziati provides a comprehensive overview of cataclysmic variables (CVs), binary systems where a white dwarf accretes matter from a companion star. The paper discusses the classification of CVs (classical novae, recurrent novae, dwarf novae, and nova-like objects), emphasizing the distinction between non-magnetic systems and magnetic CVs (Polars and Intermediate Polars). It examines the multifrequency emission properties across the electromagnetic spectrum, from radio to gamma rays, and addresses the evolving understanding of the orbital period gap. The review highlights renewed interest in CVs driven by high-energy detections (particularly by INTEGRAL) and explores their role as potential progenitors of Type Ia supernovae. The authors advocate for viewing CVs as gravimagnetic rotators and identify several open questions regarding classification, accretion disc physics, and system evolution.
