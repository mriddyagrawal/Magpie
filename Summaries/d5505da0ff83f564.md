# The evolutionary status of Cataclysmic Variables: eclipse modelling of 15 systems

This paper presents precise component mass measurements for 15 eclipsing cataclysmic variables (CVs) derived via eclipse modelling of high-time-resolution ULTRACAM and ULTRASPEC photometry, yielding 6 new and 9 improved estimates. The authors update the calibration between superhump period excess and mass ratio to infer donor properties for 225 superhumping systems, confirming high average white dwarf masses but finding no correlation with orbital period. They argue for a revised orbital period minimum of 79.6±0.2 minutes and demonstrate that donors below the period gap exhibit minimal intrinsic scatter in radius (0.005 R⊙), indicating a tight evolutionary track. Their analysis suggests standard angular momentum loss models fit donors just below the gap but require additional mechanisms to reproduce the observed period minimum and radii at lower masses.
