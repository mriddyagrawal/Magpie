# MTH-245 Handout 8: One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)

This educational handout introduces one-way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) as a parametric method for comparing means across three or more populations (t ≥ 3), generalizing the two-sample t-test. It develops the theoretical foundation by decomposing total variation into between-sample (SS_sample) and within-sample (SS_res) sums of squares, deriving the F-test statistic as the ratio of mean squares (MS_sample/MS_res) to test H₀: µ₁ = µ₂ = ... = µ_t. The document details the five-step hypothesis testing procedure, conditions for use (independence, normality, homogeneity of variances), and effect size calculation using omega-squared (ω²). A complete case study demonstrates the method by evaluating whether the serum biomarker MFAP4 differs across five stages of hepatitis C-induced liver fibrosis (Desmet-Scheuer stages 0-4) using R statistical software.
