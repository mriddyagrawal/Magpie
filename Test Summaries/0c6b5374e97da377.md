# MTH-245 Handout 8: One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)

This educational handout introduces one-way ANOVA as a parametric method for testing equality of means across three or more populations (t ≥ 3). It develops the theoretical foundation by partitioning total variation into between-sample (SSsample) and within-sample (SSres) components, deriving the F-test statistic as the ratio of mean squares (MSsample/MSres). The document provides computational formulas for sums of squares and mean squares, explains the F-distribution with (t-1, N-t) degrees of freedom, and outlines the five-step hypothesis testing procedure including effect size calculation using omega-squared (ω²). It summarizes conditions for use (independence, normality, homogeneity of variances, equal sample sizes) and notes the procedure's robustness to mild violations. A complete worked example analyzes MFAP4 biomarker levels across five hepatitis C fibrosis stages using R code, demonstrating practical implementation and interpretation despite noted violations of normality and equal variance assumptions.
