# MTH-245 Handout 0: Foundations of Statistical Analysis and Inference

This academic handout reviews core concepts from introductory statistics for an intermediate course (MTH-245), defining statistics as the science of data used to inform evidence-based decisions. It presents a rigorous seven-step framework for conducting data analysis, from translating broad research questions into precise statistical questions about population parameters, through sampling and data collection, to performing statistical inference via hypothesis testing and confidence intervals. The document emphasizes critical distinctions between statistical significance (which the author prefers to call 'statistically discernible') and practical effect size, illustrating these concepts with an NFL playoff home-field advantage example. It concludes with the Central Limit Theorem, explaining its role in enabling inference about population means and proportions regardless of the underlying population distribution shape, provided specific sample size conditions are met.
