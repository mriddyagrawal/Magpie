# MTH 245 Handout 14: Transformations and Standardization in Regression Analysis

This educational handout presents techniques for addressing violations of linear regression assumptions, specifically non-normality and non-constant variance of residuals. It demonstrates log-transforming response variables and squaring quantitative predictor variables to ameliorate diagnostic issues, illustrated with R code, simulated data examples, and comparisons of R-squared and adjusted R-squared statistics. The document also covers standardization (centering and scaling) of predictors to facilitate coefficient comparison and interpretation, noting that this procedure preserves significance tests and goodness-of-fit measures while converting coefficients to standard deviation units.
