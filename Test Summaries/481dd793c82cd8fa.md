# Artwork Analysis: Plato's Educational System and the Path of the Philosopher Ruler

This document analyzes an artwork that uses contemporary flowchart symbols—rectangles, parallelograms, and diamonds—to visualize the rigorous, merit-based educational journey prescribed by Plato for Philosopher Rulers, interpreting the Allegory of the Cave as both a critique and extension of his ideal city. The artwork maps progression through stages of abstract learning, including a five-year Dialectic course, with failures diverting to practical roles such as Harmon-ists, Astronomers, or lower guardians, while noting textual ambiguities regarding candidates who fail the Dialectician's assessment. The analysis emphasizes the system's absence of nepotism and gender bias, its privileging of abstract reasoning over practical application, and draws parallels to modern liberal arts education and meritocratic structures like the US college system.
