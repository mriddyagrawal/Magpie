# MTH 245 Handout 10: Mood's Median Test for Multiple Samples

This statistics course handout introduces Mood's Median Test as a nonparametric alternative to one-way ANOVA for comparing population medians across t independent groups when normality or equal variance assumptions are violated. The document outlines a five-step hypothesis testing framework utilizing a chi-squared test statistic with t-1 degrees of freedom, based on observed and expected counts of observations falling above and below the grand median. It applies this method to a study of hippocampal volume differences among college football players with concussions, without concussions, and healthy controls (Singh et al., 2014), providing complete R code (using RVAideMemoire, tidyverse, and effectsize packages) for data exploration, visualization, test execution, and effect size calculation (Cramer's V).
