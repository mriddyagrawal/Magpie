# MTH-245 Handout 6: Statistical Inference for Difference of Two Proportions

This academic handout provides a comprehensive guide to statistical inference for comparing two population proportions (p₁ − p₂), covering both parametric and nonparametric methods. It presents the Central Limit Theorem for the sampling distribution of the difference in sample proportions, detailing conditions for approximate normality and standard error calculations using both separate and pooled estimates. The document outlines a rigorous five-step large-sample z-test procedure for hypothesis testing, including the calculation of Cohen's h for effect size measurement. It also derives the formula for large-sample confidence intervals and introduces percentile bootstrap confidence intervals as a robust nonparametric alternative when sample size conditions are violated. A detailed R-based case study comparing Hepatitis B virus infection rates between male and female Irish prisoners illustrates practical implementation, including warnings about validity when the shape condition (n₂p̃ ≈ 2.55 < 5) is not fully satisfied.
