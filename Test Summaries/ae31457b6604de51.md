# MTH-245 Handout 6: Statistical Inference for Difference of Two Proportions

This educational handout provides a comprehensive guide to statistical inference for comparing two population proportions ($p_1 - p_2$), covering both parametric and nonparametric approaches. It establishes the Central Limit Theorem for sampling distributions of proportion differences, including the 'shape condition' requirements ($n_i p_i > 5$) and standard error derivations. The document details a five-step hypothesis testing framework (z-test) utilizing pooled proportions under the null hypothesis, alongside Cohen's h for effect size quantification. It derives the mathematical formulation for large-sample confidence intervals and introduces percentile bootstrap methods as a robust alternative when asymptotic assumptions are questionable. A detailed case study analyzes Hepatitis B virus infection rates between male and female Irish prisoners (Long et al., 2001), providing complete R code implementations using `prop.test()`, manual calculations, and bootstrap resampling procedures.
