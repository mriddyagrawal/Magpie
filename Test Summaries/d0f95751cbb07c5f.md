# DataFest Schedule (April 4-6)

This document details the three-day itinerary for DataFest, a data analysis competition held April 4-6 across multiple campus venues. The event kicks off Friday evening at Furman Hall Room 214 with check-in, pizza, and a data reveal session. Saturday features dedicated work time from 9 AM to 5 PM across Furman Hall, Johns Hall, and Duke Library, with provided breakfast and Chipotle lunch. The competition culminates Sunday at the Watkins Room in Trone Center with presentation submissions, brunch, judging, and winner announcements.
