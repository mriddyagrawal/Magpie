# MTH 245 Handout 4: Statistical Inference for Difference in Two Population Means

This educational handout provides a comprehensive guide to statistical inference for comparing two independent population means (μ₁ - μ₂). It details parametric methods, including the Central Limit Theorem for sampling distributions of mean differences, Welch's two-sample t-test for hypothesis testing (utilizing the Welch-Satterthwaite approximation for degrees of freedom), and confidence interval construction under assumptions of unequal variances. The document also covers nonparametric percentile bootstrap confidence intervals as a robust alternative for interval estimation. Key practical components include calculating effect sizes using Hedge's g, checking shape conditions for validity, and a complete R-based case study analyzing Spotify track danceability data comparing The Front Bottoms and Manchester Orchestra to demonstrate both parametric and bootstrap inference procedures.
