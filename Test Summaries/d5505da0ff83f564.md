# The evolutionary status of Cataclysmic Variables: eclipse modelling of 15 systems

This study presents precise component mass measurements for 15 eclipsing Cataclysmic Variables (CVs) derived from high-time-resolution photometry using ULTRACAM and ULTRASPEC. The authors calibrate the relationship between superhump period excess and mass ratio to estimate donor properties for 225 systems, confirming a high average white dwarf mass of 0.81 M⊙ but finding no trend with orbital period. Analysis reveals that CV donors below the period gap exhibit an intrinsic scatter of only 0.005 R⊙ around a common evolutionary track, implying minimal variation in angular momentum loss (AML) rates, and argues for a revised orbital period minimum of 79.6 ± 0.2 minutes. While standard evolutionary tracks reasonably fit donor masses just below the period gap without additional AML, they fail to reproduce the observed period minimum or donor radii below 0.1 M⊙.
