# MTH-245 Handout 1: Statistical Inference for a Population Mean

This educational handout covers statistical inference methods for a population mean (µ), including the Central Limit Theorem, parametric t-tests, and interval estimation. It distinguishes between parametric approaches (which assume normal distributions) and nonparametric methods such as bootstrap resampling, providing the five-step procedure for hypothesis testing using the t-statistic and formulas for calculating effect sizes (Hedge's g). The document derives both parametric t-confidence intervals and percentile bootstrap confidence intervals, illustrating these concepts with an extended R-based example analyzing pH levels from a sample of 53 Florida lakes to test if the mean is less than 7.
