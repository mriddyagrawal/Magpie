MAGPIE — 1-YEAR ACTION PLAN
(Format: Arial 11pt, line spacing 1.5, 1 page)

PROJECT
Magpie — a local-first AI that reads every file on your laptop and answers any question in plain English. Private by default. Sovereign forever. Wedge: solopreneurs and freelancers running their entire business out of a folder.


PHASES & TIMELINE

Months 1–3 | MVP Build.
Ship the core engine: local indexing, two-layer summary architecture (summarize first, descend into source only on a hit), and a desktop client on macOS. First killer feature working — "Who hasn't paid me?" answered against a freelancer's invoice + bank statement folder, with citations to each source file.

Months 4–6 | Closed Beta + Iteration.
Recruit 100 beta users from Indie Hackers, Twitter, and r/freelance. Iterate hard on UX from real usage. Ship Windows. Wire up Stripe billing. Launch the public site.

Months 7–9 | Public Launch.
Product Hunt, Hacker News, YouTube demos of the killer question. Goal: 1,000 paying users by Month 9.

Months 10–12 | Scale + Protocol Foundation.
Reach 5,000–10,000 paying users. Begin building the SDK and browser extension that let other apps query the local Magpie index — laying groundwork for Wave 2 (the personal-AI protocol).

FUNDING MODEL

Bootstrap → MVP (Months 1–3): founder capital + ~$25K friends-and-family angel.
Pre-seed (Month 4): $300K–$500K at $5–8M valuation. 18-month runway + first key hire.
Seed (post-launch, ~Month 12–18): $2–4M at $15–25M valuation, on the back of revenue traction.


BUSINESS MODEL

Individual:  $29/month or $499 lifetime.
Pro:         $99/month per seat — accountants, bookkeepers, and freelance-ops teams managing multiple client folders.
Year 2+:     SDK / protocol licensing for third-party apps and websites that integrate with Magpie's local index.


YEAR-1 REVENUE GOAL

Bear:  1,000 paying users     →  $350K ARR
Base:  5,000 paying users     →  $1.7M ARR
Bull: 10,000 paying users     →  $3M ARR

The base case puts Magpie on Cursor's exact year-one trajectory at comparable customer-acquisition cost: a vertical AI answer engine, on the user's own machine, growing on word of mouth in a small loud community.
